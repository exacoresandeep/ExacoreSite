How We Helped a Growing Enterprise Succeed with Oracle Fusion Cloud

We helped a fast-growing organization bring finance, procurement and operations onto its first enterprise-wide ERP platform, then stayed on to support the system after go-live.

ORACLE Fusion Cloud | Financials, Procurement & Projects
Enterprise ERP | Implementation & managed services

Client: An anonymized growing enterprise with multiple business units and an expanding customer base
Platform: Oracle Fusion Cloud
Scope: Finance, procurement and operations
Integrations: Banking, payroll and CRM through Oracle Integration Cloud
Services: Discovery, implementation, go-live hypercare and Oracle Managed Services

Meeting the client

The business was growing quickly, with several business units and an expanding customer base. Leadership chose Oracle Fusion Cloud as its first enterprise-wide ERP platform: one modern system for finance, procurement and operations, built to support the organization as it grew.

The team needed a partner who could design, deploy and support the platform from day one. They had limited in-house enterprise application experience and wanted a foundation that could scale without creating unnecessary complexity.

What they were up against

Before Fusion, the organization relied on basic accounting tools and spreadsheets. As the business expanded, disconnected systems and manual work made it harder to operate and get a timely view of results.

  • Finance, purchasing and project information sat in separate tools.
  • Leadership waited days for consolidated financial reporting.
  • Purchase requests and invoices moved through email, creating audit and compliance risk.
  • Adding an entity, currency or location required significant manual effort.
  • The organization had limited in-house enterprise ERP expertise.

Discovery and Oracle Fusion Consulting

We began with discovery workshops involving finance, procurement and operations leaders. Through our Oracle Fusion Consulting services, we documented requirements, defined the enterprise structure and designed processes around Oracle leading practices.

Early decisions covered which modules to start with, which customizations to retain and the readiness of the client's data. We kept customization to a minimum to help the organization benefit from Oracle's quarterly updates.

Implementation in phases

We delivered the implementation through clear phases, building a scalable foundation and preparing teams for go-live.

01

Foundation design

Established ledgers, chart of accounts, business units and security roles to support long-term growth.

02

Module configuration

Configured Financials, Procurement and Project Management to support the organization's workflows.

03

Integration

Connected banking, payroll and CRM systems through Oracle Integration Cloud.

04

Data loading

Validated and loaded opening balances, supplier and customer records, and item data using structured templates.

05

Testing and training

Ran multiple test cycles and user acceptance testing, then delivered role-based training for each team.

06

Go-live and hypercare

Went live on schedule, with a dedicated hypercare team supporting users through their first close cycles.

Staying on after go-live: Oracle Managed Services

With no internal ERP team, the client chose our Oracle Managed Services to keep the platform stable and current. We provide:

  • 24x7 monitoring and incident management.
  • Quarterly update testing and regression support.
  • Enhancements, reports and dashboard improvements.
  • User, role and security administration.
  • Proactive advice on new Oracle Fusion features.

This gives the client enterprise-grade support without the cost of building a large IT department.

What the client gained

  • One source of truth across finance, procurement and projects.
  • A faster month-end close through automated accounting and real-time dashboards.
  • Stronger internal controls with approval workflows and audit trails.
  • A scalable foundation for adding entities, currencies and locations.
  • Predictable support costs with expert coverage after go-live.

What we took away

Starting with Oracle Fusion Cloud lets a growing organization build on best practices from the beginning, rather than retrofitting processes later. Projects run more smoothly when module scope, customization and data readiness are addressed early. Thoughtful design and a partner who stays engaged after go-live are just as important.
