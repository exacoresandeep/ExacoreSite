ORACLE EBS SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Oracle EBS implementation, migration, support and integrations.

What industries does Exacore specialize in for Oracle EBS implementations?

We have over a decade of domain expertise delivering Oracle EBS services across pharmaceuticals, chemicals, manufacturing (discrete and process), banking, and food & beverage industries. Our team understands industry-specific compliance requirements, multi-plant operations, and complex supply chain workflows unique to these sectors, enabling us to deliver tailored Oracle EBS solutions aligned with each organization’s operational and business needs.

How long does a typical Oracle EBS implementation or rollout take?

Implementation timelines for Oracle EBS implementation services vary based on scope, number of modules, and business complexity. A standard single-site implementation typically ranges from 4 to 8 months, while complex multi-country or multi-plant rollouts are phased according to business requirements and operational priorities, helping minimize disruption to ongoing operations and ensure a smooth transition to the Oracle EBS environment.

Can Exacore handle multi-country and multi-plant EBS rollouts?

Yes. We specialize in complex multi-plant and multi-entity deployments. Our rollouts ensure standardized global processes while accommodating local tax structures, statutory requirements, and multi-currency and multi-language compliance across different regions.

What is involved in migrating Oracle EBS to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)?

Our Oracle EBS to Fusion migration service enables organizations to transition their on-premise EBS environment to Oracle Cloud with a focus on security, performance, and business continuity. We manage the entire migration lifecycle—from secure network architecture design and dual-node production builds to data migration, integration validation, and rigorous testing—helping ensure minimal cutover downtime and a smooth transition to Oracle Fusion Cloud.

How do you minimize downtime during EBS cloud migration or upgrades?

We employ proven migration strategies, including optimized database cutover techniques, Oracle Data Guard setup, and pre-cutover dry runs. This allows us to perform the final transition during off-peak hours or over a weekend, helping keep critical business operations running with minimal disruption.

Can Exacore integrate Oracle EBS with our existing third-party software?

Yes. We have extensive experience interfacing Oracle EBS with third-party systems, including Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), Hospital Information Systems (HIS), core banking platforms, custom CRMs, and enterprise middleware via REST/SOAP APIs and custom interfaces.

Do you support India GST and local statutory compliance requirements?

Absolutely. We provide dedicated support and regular patch updates to keep your Oracle EBS system aligned with Indian GST, e-invoicing, e-way bills, and evolving statutory tax regulations.

What Disaster Recovery (DR) and High Availability (HA) solutions do you offer for EBS?

We implement enterprise-grade DR and HA architectures using Oracle Data Guard, tiered backup strategies, real-time data replication, and automated failover capabilities to support continuous business operations and rapid recovery in the event of an outage.

What options are available for post-implementation support and stabilization?

We offer flexible Oracle EBS support services through long-term support models, including 24/7 managed support, on-demand functional and technical assistance, system health checks, patch management, and performance tuning. Our support services help keep your Oracle EBS environment secure, stable, and running efficiently after go-live while minimizing business disruption.

Can you optimize or tune the performance of an existing Oracle EBS system?

Yes. We conduct comprehensive EBS health checks to identify system bottlenecks, query inefficiencies, custom code issues, and database performance gaps. Our team then implements targeted tuning strategies to improve response times and overall system health.
