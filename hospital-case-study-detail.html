How a Hospital Group Cut Its Oracle EBS Month-End Close from 12 Days to 6

Slow month-end close in Oracle EBS? It's often the system, not your team. See how one health check found the real bottlenecks and cut the close in half.

ORACLE E-Business Suite R12.2
Healthcare | Performance tuning

If your finance team works late every month-end, you're not alone. This multi-specialty hospital group sent thousands of billing entries from its Hospital Information System (HIS) into Oracle EBS R12.2 every day, and most of them piled up in the last week of the month. Leadership assumed the team was slow and started planning to hire more people.

Our Oracle EBS health check told a different story. Stuck concurrent programs, reports that ran for hours and reconciliation differences found far too late were the real culprits. Within two closing cycles, the month-end close dropped from 12 working days to 6, the longest report went from about 4 hours to 25 minutes, and the headcount plan was shelved.

This case study will help you

  • See whether your slow month-end close is a system problem rather than a people problem.
  • Find the concurrent program bottlenecks that leave Auto Invoice, Create Accounting and Transfer to GL stuck in "Pending".
  • Learn how one custom report went from about 4 hours to 25 minutes.
  • Catch HIS-to-AR and subledger-to-GL reconciliation differences early, not in the last few days of the close.
  • Set up a close calendar with a named owner for every module and branch.
  • Make the case for fixing Oracle EBS performance before you add headcount.
