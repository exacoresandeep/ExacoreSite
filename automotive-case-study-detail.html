Oracle EBS Implementation for Manufacturers: Live in 6 Months

See how an automotive components manufacturer replaced disconnected systems and spreadsheets with one ERP across all its plants, and cut its month-end close from 15+ days to 5–6.

ORACLE E-Business Suite | Finance & SCM
Manufacturing | Go-live in 6 months

Running several plants on separate systems and spreadsheets is expensive. This mid-size automotive components manufacturer had no real-time inventory visibility, purchase approvals that held up production, and a month-end close that dragged past 15 days. Product costs were estimates, so margins were hard to trust, and the shop floor had too much stock in some places and shortages in others.

We implemented Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) Finance and Supply Chain across every plant in 6 months, using an Oracle AIM/OUM-aligned approach with minimal customization. The go-live ran with zero production downtime. Today the financial close takes 5–6 days, inventory carrying cost is about 25% lower, the procure-to-pay cycle is about 40% faster, and the team sees inventory in real time across all plants.
