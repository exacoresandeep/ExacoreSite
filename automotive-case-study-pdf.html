How an Automotive Manufacturer Unified Finance and Supply Chain with Oracle EBS

See how a mid-size automotive components manufacturer replaced disconnected systems and spreadsheets with one ERP across all its plants, and went live in 6 months.

Industry: Manufacturing (Automotive Components)
Modules: Oracle EBS Finance and SCM
Go-live: 6 Months

The Challenge

A mid-size automotive components manufacturer was running multiple plants on disconnected systems and spreadsheets. The impact was costly:

  • No real-time inventory visibility across plants
  • Manual purchase approvals delaying production
  • Month-end close taking over 15 days
  • Inaccurate product costing and unclear margins
  • Excess stock alongside frequent material shortages

They needed a single ERP solution for manufacturing to control costs and support growth.

The Solution

We implemented Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) across all plants, covering:

  • Finance: General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management
  • SCM: Inventory, Purchasing, Order Management, Bills of Material, Work in Process, Cost Management

Our Implementation Approach: Live in 6 Months

We followed a structured, Oracle AIM/OUM-aligned methodology to reduce risk and accelerate go-live.

  1. Discovery and Business Process Study: We mapped as-is processes across procurement, production, inventory, and finance to define scope and pain points.
  2. Solution Design and Gap Analysis: Fit-gap analysis and to-be process design, including chart of accounts and organization structure, kept customization to a minimum.
  3. Configuration and Customization: We configured all modules, approval workflows, and costing methods, and built custom reports and integrations with MES and banking systems.
  4. Data Migration: Item masters, suppliers, customers, opening balances, and open orders were cleansed and migrated through multiple mock loads for accuracy.
  5. Testing (SIT/UAT): End-to-end testing of Procure-to-Pay, Order-to-Cash, and Plan-to-Produce cycles ensured business readiness.
  6. Training and Change Management: Role-based training and super-user enablement drove strong user adoption.
  7. Go-Live and Hypercare: A phased cutover with eight weeks of dedicated support delivered zero production downtime.
  8. Support and Optimization: Ongoing AMS and performance tuning continue to add value after launch.

Key success factors: Executive sponsorship, a joint project team, weekly milestone governance, and early involvement of business users.
