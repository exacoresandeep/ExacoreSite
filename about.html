About Us

Empowering Your Business Future
With Technology

Exacore IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is an Oracle-focused ERP consultancy with over a decade of experience delivering complex, mission-critical implementations across industries. We specialize in Oracle E-Business Suite, Oracle Fusion Cloud, with dedicated teams organized by technology and domain — spanning Finance, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, HCM, and Value Chain Planning.

Our track record includes ERP implementations for organizations across pharmaceuticals, chemicals, banking, food & beverage, healthcare, and manufacturing — from a 100-day Oracle ERP rollout to large multi-plant, multi-country deployments for clients such as Thermax. We've been recognized among the top 25 ERP & CRM implementers by The CEO Magazine (2020) and named one of India's 20 most promising Oracle ERP companies by The Silicon Review (2016).

Backed by a team of Oracle certified consultants, a dedicated PMO, and an in-house Training & Quality department, we're committed to delivering solutions on time and on budget — building on long-term client relationships rather than short-term wins.
