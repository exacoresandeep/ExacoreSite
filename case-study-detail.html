Understanding the iExpense module

The iExpense module in Oracle ERP enables employees to efficiently submit and manage their business expenses. It provides a centralized platform for recording expenses, submitting reports, obtaining approvals, and monitoring reimbursement status.

Key Features

01

Expense Submission

Employees can quickly submit their business expenses through an intuitive interface.

02

Automated Approval

Automated workflows route expense reports to the appropriate managers for approval.

03

Real-time Tracking

Employees and managers can monitor expense status throughout the approval process.

04

Reporting

Detailed reports provide better visibility into organizational spending.
