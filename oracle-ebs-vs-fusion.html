ORACLE ERP STRATEGY

Stay on Oracle EBS or Move to Fusion? A Practical Guide for Finance and IT Leaders

Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) R12 supports finance, supply chain, manufacturing and HR operations worldwide. Meanwhile, Oracle Fusion Cloud offers modern ERP capabilities, embedded analytics, AI features and regular updates.

The right choice depends on your business requirements, customization needs, budget and modernization goals. This guide compares the considerations to help finance and IT leaders plan their next step.

Oracle ERP InsightsBy the Exacore Team

Why This Decision Matters

Oracle EBS and Oracle Fusion Cloud can both support complex enterprise operations, but they represent different technology and operating models. The best decision is the one that aligns with your business priorities, current investments and long-term modernization goals.

The Case for Staying on Oracle EBS

Staying on Oracle EBS makes sense for organizations with stable systems and significant investments in existing customizations. Consider staying if:

  • Customizations are critical. Oracle Forms and Reports, Oracle OAF and Oracle Workflow support essential business processes.
  • Recent investments exist. Your organization has recently completed an Oracle ERP upgrade.
  • Budget is limited. Phased modernization helps spread costs and reduce disruption.
  • Continuity matters. Existing processes work reliably and migration risks outweigh immediate benefits.

Oracle EBS 12.2 has a long support runway, with Premier Support continuing through at least 2037, subject to Oracle's published policies. Organizations can modernize EBS using Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC) and Oracle Analytics Cloud. Regular patching, Oracle DBA services and Oracle EBS support remain essential.

The Case for Moving to Oracle Fusion Cloud

Oracle Fusion ERP suits organizations seeking scalability, standardized processes and reduced infrastructure management. Consider migrating if you want to:

  • Reduce hardware maintenance and infrastructure administration.
  • Adopt standardized business processes instead of recreating legacy customizations.
  • Access continuous innovation, analytics and AI capabilities.
  • Scale operations across new geographies and business units.
  • Develop cloud ERP capabilities and simplify long-term technology management.

An Oracle EBS to Fusion migration involves business transformation, data preparation, integration planning and change management—not simply a technical upgrade.

Key Factors to Consider

Before deciding, evaluate these factors:

  1. Total cost of ownership. Compare infrastructure, licensing, support and staffing against Fusion subscriptions and implementation costs.
  2. Customizations. Identify extensions that can be retired, replaced or rebuilt.
  3. Data and integrations. Assess connections with banks, tax systems, CRM and manufacturing applications.
  4. Business readiness. Consider employee training, stakeholder support and process changes.
  5. Industry requirements. Ensure Oracle Financials, Oracle SCM, Oracle Procurement and Oracle Manufacturing meet your needs.
  6. Support and skills. Evaluate Oracle managed services, application support and specialist staffing.

A Phased Modernization Approach

A practical roadmap can reduce disruption while keeping modernization aligned with business value:

  1. Assess. Conduct an Oracle ERP health check and review customizations and integrations.
  2. Stabilize. Address EBS performance, security and maintenance.
  3. Modernize. Introduce OCI, OIC or analytics capabilities where appropriate.
  4. Migrate selectively. Move suitable modules to Fusion based on business priorities.
  5. Optimize. Retire legacy components as the new environment delivers value.

Which Option Is Right for You?

Stay on Oracle EBS if stability, valuable customizations and cost control are priorities. Choose Oracle Fusion Cloud if scalability, innovation and process standardization matter more.

For many enterprises, optimizing EBS today while planning a phased Fusion migration offers a balanced approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Oracle EBS R12 still supported?

Oracle states that Premier Support for EBS 12.2 continues through at least 2037, subject to its published policies.

How long does migration take?

Timelines depend on modules, integrations, data volumes and customization complexity.

Can EBS and Fusion run together?

Yes. Hybrid environments can connect applications through Oracle Integration Cloud.

Is post-migration support necessary?

Yes. Application management helps maintain integrations, reporting, quarterly update readiness and user support.
